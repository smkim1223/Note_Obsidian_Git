# Dual Brønsted–Lewis Acid Catalysts (Superacids)

| Catalyst / System | Composition & Nature | Brønsted–Lewis Duality | Typical Properties / Uses |
|------------------|----------------------|------------------------|---------------------------|
| **Fluoroantimonic acid (HSbF₆)** | HF + SbF₅ mixture → [H₂F]⁺[SbF₆]⁻ | Brønsted: HF donates H⁺; Lewis: SbF₅ accepts F⁻/e⁻ | pKₐ < –20. Protonates alkanes, initiates polymerizations, isomerizations, alkylations. Extremely corrosive. |
| **Magic Acid (FSO₃H + SbF₅)** | Fluorosulfonic acid + antimony pentafluoride | Brønsted: FSO₃H; Lewis: SbF₅ | pKₐ ~ –23. Stabilizes carbocations. Used in strong acid catalysis and mechanistic studies. |
| **Olah’s Superacid (HF + SbF₅)** | Variant of HSbF₆ under controlled conditions | Same dual acidity | Ultra-high acidity. Used for superacid chemistry research. |
| **Fluorosulfonic acid (FSO₃H)** | Pure Brønsted acid, but with Lewis acids becomes superacid | Alone Brønsted; with SbF₅ dual | Catalyst for isomerization, alkylation, nitration. Less corrosive than HSbF₆. |
| **HF + BF₃ (Boron trifluoride–HF)** | Hydrogen fluoride plus boron trifluoride | Brønsted: HF; Lewis: BF₃ | Forms [H₂F]⁺[BF₄]⁻. Strong but less extreme than HSbF₆. Industrial alkylation catalyst. |
| **Triflic acid + Lewis acids (TfOH + SbF₅ or AlCl₃)** | Trifluoromethanesulfonic acid with strong Lewis acids | Dual | TfOH alone pKₐ –14; with SbF₅ superacidic. Used in fine chemical synthesis. |
| **Chlorosulfonic acid + Lewis acids** | e.g. ClSO₃H + AlCl₃ | Dual | Highly corrosive. Nitrations, sulfonations. |
| **Solid Superacids (e.g. SO₄²⁻/ZrO₂, WO₃/ZrO₂)** | Sulfate groups on oxides provide Brønsted sites; metal centers provide Lewis sites | Surface has both | Catalyze isomerization of alkanes, esterifications, acylations at milder conditions. Easier to handle than liquid superacids. |
| **Mixed Metal Halide Acids (HF–TaF₅, HF–NbF₅)** | HF with tantalum or niobium pentafluoride | Brønsted HF + Lewis metal fluoride | Similar to SbF₅ superacids but different halide tolerance. Used mainly in research. |

---

## General Traits
- **Extraordinary acidity (pKₐ < –10)**: Can protonate very weak bases like alkanes.
- **Dual function:** Brønsted sites donate H⁺; Lewis sites stabilize anions or accept electrons.
- **Applications:** Alkylation, isomerization, polymerization, electrophilic substitutions, carbocation stabilization.
- **Handling:** Highly corrosive, moisture-sensitive. Liquid superacids need special containers (PTFE, special alloys).
- **Industrial vs Lab:** Liquid superacids mainly for research; solid superacids mimic dual sites in a safer form for industrial catalysis.
